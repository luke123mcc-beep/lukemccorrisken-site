Who I Am

I'm Luke McCorrisken. I help ambitious, impact-led founders get unstuck — by working on the business and the person running it, at the same time.

Most of my work happens where a business decision and a founder's own pattern turn out to be the same problem wearing two faces. I diagnose both, and I stay in the work with you until the roadmap is real.

I'm also building Daily Founder, an AI-personalised learning platform for early-stage founders — so this isn't theory. It's the work I do every day, on the businesses I run myself.
